		NAIVE BAYES

STEP 1) FROM THE TABLE

	No.of Students	=	10

	Maths - High	=	5
	Maths - Low	=	5

	Attendance - Good	=	5
	Attendance - Poor	=	5

	Internal - High	=	2
	Internal - Medium	= 	5
	Internal - Low	=	3

	Pass		=	6
	Fail		=	4

STEP 2) GOAL----> Prediction for NEW Student

	Maths = High
	Attendance = Poor
	Internal = Medium

STEP 3) HOW TO SOLVE

	3)A) Find CLASS PROBABILITIES

	P(PASS) = 6/10	=	0.6

	P(FAIL) = 4/10	=	0.4

STEP 4) FIND CONDITIONAL PROBABILITIES

	4)A)	FOR PASS

	P(MATHS = HIGH |PASS)
	P(ATTENDANCE = POOR |PASS)
	P(INTERNAL = MEDIUM |PASS)

	4)B)	FOR FAIL

STEP 5) COUNT VALUES

	5)A) FOR PASS(TOTALLY 6 STUDENTS)

	Maths = High	=	5
	Attendance = Poor	=	2
	Internal = Medium	=	3

	5)B) FOR FAIL(TOTALLY 4 STUDENTS)

	Maths = High	=	0
	Attendance = Poor	=	3
	Internal = Medium	=	1

STEP 6) APPLY LAPLACE SMOOTHING----> +1

	CATEGORY

		6)A) Maths	= 2(High,Low)
		6)B) Attendance	= 2(Good,Poor)
		6)C) Internal	= 3(High,Medium,Poor)

STEP 7) FOR PASS

	7)A) P(Maths = High |Pass) 	= (5+1)/(6+2)	=	6/8	= 0.75
	7)B) P(Attendance = Poor |Pass)	= (2+1)/(6+2)	=	3/8	= 0.375
	7)C) P(Internal = Medium |Pass)	= (3+1)/(6+3)	=	4/9	= 0.44

	FOR FAIL

	7)D) P(Maths = High |Fail)	= (0+1)/(4+2)	=	1/6	= 0.17
	7)E) P(Attendance = Poor | Fail)	= (3+1)/(4+2)	=	4/6	= 0.67
	7)F) P(Internal = Medium | Fail)	= (1+1)/(4+3)	=	2/7	= 0.29

STEP 8) APPLY NAIVE BAYES FORMULA

	8)A) FOR PASS

		P(PASS | DATA) = 0.6 X 0.75 X 0.375 X 0.44

		RESULT = 0.074

	8)B) FOR FAIL

		P(FAIL |DATA) = 0.4 X 0.17 X 0.67 X 0.29

		RESULT = 0.013

STEP 9) FINAL COMPARISON

	PASS	=	0.074
	FAIL	=	0.013

STEP 10) FINAL PREDICTION

	PREDICTION = PASS
